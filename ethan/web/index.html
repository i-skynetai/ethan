Privacy & permissions

The service listens on loopback. Local authentication is optional and disabled unless the service owner explicitly configures it. A configured bearer token cannot protect against another process running as the same OS user. Keep this service on your own device; remote hosting is not enabled.

Approval commands require the exact approval ID. Voice never sends a command automatically. No credentials are displayed or stored in this panel.
